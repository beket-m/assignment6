My Favorite EDM Music

Electronic Dance Music (EDM) is one of my favorite genres of music. I especially enjoy melodic house, progressive house, and songs that combine emotional melodies with energetic drops.

My Favorite EDM Artists

Some of my favorite EDM artists include Audien, John Summit, and William Black. Each artist has a unique sound and approach to producing electronic music.

My Favorite Things About EDM

  • Melodic synthesizers and chord progressions
  • Energetic buildups and drops
  • Creative sound design and production
  • Live performances and music festivals

Music Production

I am also interested in producing electronic music. I enjoy experimenting with synthesizers, melodies, and different effects to create new sounds. Music production combines creativity and technology, which makes it especially interesting to me.
